Information Technology Program

Master the art and science of computing systems to drive technological innovation.

Vision & Mission

Vision: To develop next-generation, research and innovation-led engineers.

Mission: To provide a platform for innovation where students use imparted knowledge for technological advancements and societal betterment.

Program Overview

Established in 2006, the Information Technology Department at Brahma Valley Polytechnic is a sister branch of Computer Engineering. Our 3-year diploma program equips students with expertise in computing systems, from mobile applications to supercomputers, focusing on scientific and engineering principles.

  • Duration: 3 Years
  • Eligibility: 10th Pass (min. 35% marks)
  • Mode: Full-Time
  • Intake: 120 Seats

Key Features

Digital Classrooms

Dedicated Internet Lab

Industry Interaction

Expert Faculty

Program Objectives

  • Promote interaction with industry experts for real-world insights.
  • Encourage faculty to stay updated with emerging technologies.
  • Foster a teaching-learning process exploring technological advancements.

Curriculum Highlights

Core Subjects
  • Programming (Python, Java, C++)
  • Database Management (SQL, MySQL)
  • Networking & Cybersecurity
  • Operating Systems
Advanced Topics
  • Web Development (HTML5, CSS3, JS)
  • Cloud Computing Fundamentals
  • Mobile App Development
  • Data Analytics Basics
Practical Training
  • Software Development Projects
  • Network Configuration Labs
  • 6-Month Industry Internship
  • Capstone Project

Program Outcomes

By the end of the program, you will be able to:

Develop robust software applications

Secure and manage networks

Build web and mobile applications

Apply data analytics techniques

Mechanical Engineering Program

Master the principles of mechanical systems to drive innovation in engineering and technology.

Vision & Mission

Vision: To cultivate innovative mechanical engineers who lead advancements in technology and industry.

Mission: To provide a dynamic learning environment that equips students with practical skills and theoretical knowledge for designing, analyzing, and maintaining mechanical systems, contributing to societal progress.

Program Overview

The Mechanical Engineering Department at Brahma Valley College of Technical Education, a prestigious and outstanding department, boasts a 100% admission rate with an intake of 180 students. With 15 dedicated, young, and energetic staff members, the department achieves exceptional academic results (100% in final year, ~90% in second year). The 3-year diploma program equips students with expertise in mechanical systems, manufacturing, and design.

  • Duration: 3 Years
  • Eligibility: 10th Pass (min. 35% marks)
  • Mode: Full-Time
  • Intake: 180 Seats

Key Features

State-of-the-Art Labs

Program Objectives

  • Equip students with practical skills for mechanical system design and maintenance.
  • Foster innovation through hands-on projects and industry collaboration.
  • Prepare students for professional roles in manufacturing and engineering.

Program Outcomes

By the end of the program, you will be able to:

Design mechanical components

Operate and maintain machinery

Apply CNC programming techniques

Implement quality control measures

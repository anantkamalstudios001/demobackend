Electrical Engineering Program

Master the principles of electrical systems to drive innovation in power and technology.

Vision & Mission

Vision: To cultivate innovative electrical engineers who lead advancements in power and technology.

Mission: To provide a dynamic learning environment that equips students with skills for analysis, implementation, and maintenance of electrical systems, contributing to societal progress.

Program Overview

Established in 2009, the Electrical Engineering Department at Brahma Valley Polytechnic focuses on the study of electrical motors, transformers, switch gears, and electricity generation, transmission, distribution, and utilization. This 3-year diploma program equips students with skills for analysis, system implementation, and maintenance of electrical equipment.

  • Duration: 3 Years
  • Eligibility: 10th Pass (min. 35% marks)
  • Mode: Full-Time
  • Intake: 60 Seats

Program Objectives

  • Equip students with practical skills for electrical system maintenance and operation.
  • Foster innovation through industry-aligned projects and training.
  • Prepare students for professional roles in power generation and distribution.

Program Outcomes

By the end of the program, you will be able to:

Design and maintain electrical circuits

Operate and test electrical machines

Implement power distribution systems

Apply renewable energy solutions

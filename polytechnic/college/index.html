About BVCTE

Welcome to Brahma Valley College of Technical Education (BVCTE), a premier institute built with a vision to produce technical personnel of international caliber who will lead India’s technological advancements.

BVCTE has quickly established itself as a leader in technical education. Located in the serene surroundings of Anjaneri near Nashik City, our 35-acre campus at the foot of the Sahyadri Range fosters a perfect learning environment with lush greenery and thoughtfully designed infrastructure.

Learn More

Easily Approachable Campus

  • 20 KM from Nashik City on Nashik-Trimbakeshwar Highway
  • 180 KM from Aurangabad
  • 30 KM from Nashik Road Railway Station, 65 KM from Manmad Junction
  • Nearest airport: 40 KM at Ozar MIG, 180 KM at Mumbai

Our Features

  • First polytechnic in Nashik with interactive digital classrooms
  • Beautiful 35-acre campus, with 5 acres for polytechnic
  • Experienced and qualified faculty
  • Central e-library with journals and periodicals
  • Hostels: 600 boys & 400 girls, hygienic cafeteria
  • Top-class transport, sports & gym facilities
  • Industrial visits: IIT Powai, NTPC, BSNL, etc.
  • Technical seminars: automation, CAD/CAM, robotics
  • Annual cultural festival ‘Brahmotsav’
  • Wi-Fi enabled campus with 20 Mbps
  • Amphitheater & large playground

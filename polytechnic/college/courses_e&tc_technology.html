Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering Program

Master the principles of electronics and communication to drive innovation in modern technology.

Vision & Mission

Vision: To nurture innovative engineers who lead advancements in electronics and telecommunication technologies.

Mission: To provide a dynamic learning platform that equips students with skills for analysis, implementation, and maintenance of E&TC systems, contributing to technological and societal progress.

Program Overview

Established in 2006, the Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering Department at Brahma Valley Polytechnic focuses on analog and digital communication, microprocessors, satellite systems, and microwave engineering. This 3-year diploma program equips students with skills for designing, implementing, and maintaining communication and electronic systems.

  • Duration: 3 Years
  • Eligibility: 10th Pass (min. 35% marks)
  • Mode: Full-Time
  • Intake: 60 Seats

  • Develop skills for designing and maintaining communication systems.

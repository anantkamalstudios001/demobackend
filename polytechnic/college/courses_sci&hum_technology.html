Science & Humanities Program

Build a strong foundation in applied sciences, mathematics, and communication to excel in engineering and technology.

Vision & Mission

Vision: To empower students with foundational knowledge and skills that drive success in technical education and beyond.

Mission: To provide a robust platform for learning applied sciences, mathematics, and communication skills, fostering personal and professional growth for future technologists.

Program Overview

Established in 2006, the Science & Humanities Department at Brahma Valley College of Technical Education forms the first step toward technical education. With well-equipped Physics and Chemistry labs, the department teaches foundational subjects like Basic Physics, Basic Chemistry, Mathematics, English, Communication Skills, and Development of Life Skills, preparing students for all engineering disciplines.

  • Duration: Integrated in 1st & 2nd Semesters
  • Eligibility: 10th Pass (min. 35% marks)
  • Mode: Full-Time
  • Intake: Varies by Engineering Program

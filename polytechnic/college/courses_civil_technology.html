Civil Engineering Program

Master the art and science of designing and constructing vital infrastructure.

Vision & Mission

Vision: To develop innovative civil engineers who shape sustainable infrastructure for the future.

Mission: To provide a comprehensive learning platform that equips students with practical and theoretical skills for designing, constructing, and maintaining civil engineering projects, contributing to societal development.

Program Overview

Established in July 2013, the Civil Engineering Department at Brahma Valley College of Technical Education is equipped with state-of-the-art laboratories and model rooms to support learning in bridges, roads, dams, canals, and irrigation projects. This 3-year diploma program provides students with comprehensive knowledge and practical skills for infrastructure development and fieldwork.

  • Duration: 3 Years
  • Eligibility: 10th Pass (min. 35% marks)
  • Mode: Full-Time
  • Intake: 60 Seats

Key Features

Advanced Laboratories

Surveying Equipment

Model Rooms

Expert Faculty

Program Objectives

  • Develop skills for designing and constructing infrastructure projects.
  • Provide hands-on training in surveying and material testing.
  • Prepare students for fieldwork in house building and infrastructure development.

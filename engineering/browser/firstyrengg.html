Glorious Tradition of Department of Mechanical

  • The Department of Mechanical of the College was started in 1959. The first Principal of the college was famous Mechanical literary writer P.L. Deshpande.
  • The Department has a great tradition of many writers and critics, Bhalchandra Khandekar, profound scholar of saint literature Dr. R.R. Gosavi, Dr. M.S.Patil famous writers of Maharashtra have worked in the department.
  • The writers and renowned Mechanical Critics like Dr. Dilip Dhondge, Dr. Kamal Aher, Dr. Sayaji Pagar, were the Alumni of this department.
  • So far, thousands of students have consumed the nectar of Mechanical through the department and numerous students are working in high positions at various places.
  • The Research Centre of Mechanical has enrolled 18 students for Ph.D Degree today.

About the Department

For the first year, subjects are common to all courses, which include subjects like Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, FPL, Basic Electronics, Basic Electrical & Basic Mechanical Engineering. Behind the growth of all latest technologies, there is a vital role of basic sciences. The first step towards the development of any technology comprehends basic concepts of fundamental sciences. This exactly is provided by us, which helps in making a rock solid foundation for their future, in specialized fields of engineering. Knowledge of basic sciences is used to tackle many problems and give an opportunity to work on new technologies.

Engineering science department has well equipped & furnished chemistry, physics Laboratories. This department has well experienced & highly qualified staff that always encourages the students to participate in co-curricular activities. The department has implemented a mentor system for the students.

OBJECTIVE

1. To provide the students a broad based foundation in engineering for a diverse range of careers in industry, government sector and overseas employment. 2. To enable them to acquire a large range of skills and ways of pragmatic thinking drawn from the engineering technology and humanities.

Our Vision

To become a leading department where the best in research, learning, and teaching collaborate.

Our Mission

  • To provide quality technical education through academic programs.
  • To provide a career platform to the students through exposure to industry.
  • To promote excellence by encouraging creativity, soft skills improvisation and discipline.

Quality Policy

Committed to provide quality education to the students enabling them to excel in the fields of Science, Engineering & Technology to cater to the changing and challenging needs of society and industry.

Innovative Teaching Method

  • Flipped Classroom: Students study content at home (e.g., through videos), and do problem-solving or discussions in class.
  • Project-Based Learning (PBL): Students learn by working on real-world projects.
  • Gamification: Using game elements like points, levels, or challenges to motivate learning.
  • Blended Learning: Combining traditional classroom teaching with online digital media.
  • Collaborative Learning: Students work in groups to solve problems and learn from each other.
  • Use of Technology: Smartboards, AR/VR, simulations, and educational apps.

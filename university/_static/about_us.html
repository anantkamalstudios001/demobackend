About Brahma Valley University

Discover our legacy of excellence in education and innovation.

Who We Are

Brahma Valley University, located in the serene valley of Nashik, Maharashtra, is a premier institution dedicated to fostering academic excellence, innovation, and leadership. Established in 2002, we offer a diverse range of programs across Engineering, Pharmacy, MBA, Polytechnic, ITI, B.Ed, and Public School education, shaping the leaders of tomorrow.

Vision & Mission

Vision

To be a global leader in education, fostering innovation, research, and holistic development to create responsible global citizens.

Mission

  • Provide quality education with a focus on practical learning.
  • Promote research and innovation to address global challenges.
  • Nurture ethical leaders with a commitment to societal progress.

Placements

Brahma Valley University’s Placement Cell ensures our students are well-prepared for successful careers. In 2024, 92% of our graduates secured placements in top-tier companies, with an average salary package of ₹5.5 LPA.

Placement Highlights

  • 400+ companies visited campus in 2024
  • Top industries: IT, Engineering, Pharmaceuticals, Management
  • Highest package: ₹12 LPA

Innovative AI Research Breakthrough

In 2024, Brahma Valley University’s Engineering Department, led by Dr. Anil Sharma, developed an AI-driven solution for optimizing agricultural yield prediction, adopted by local farmers in Nashik. This project earned the prestigious National Innovation Award and strengthened our industry-academia collaboration.

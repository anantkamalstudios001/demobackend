Program Outcomes & Objectives

Discover the structured outcomes and objectives of our academic programs, crafted to ensure graduate success.

Explore Programs

Program Outcomes (PO)

Program Outcomes define the skills and knowledge students gain by graduation. Below are the POs for B.Tech in Computer Engineering.

PO1
Apply mathematics, science, and engineering fundamentals to solve complex problems.
PO2
Identify, formulate, and analyze engineering problems using first principles.
PO3
Design solutions for complex problems, considering societal and environmental needs.
PO4
Conduct research-based investigations to draw valid conclusions.
PO5
Utilize modern engineering tools and techniques effectively.
PO6
Evaluate societal, health, safety, and cultural impacts of engineering practices.
PO7
Understand the environmental impact of engineering solutions.
PO8
Adhere to ethical principles and professional responsibilities.
PO9
Work effectively as an individual or in multidisciplinary teams.
PO10
Communicate effectively with diverse audiences.
PO11
Apply engineering and management principles to manage projects.
PO12
Engage in continuous learning to adapt to technological changes.

Program Educational Objectives (PEO)

PEOs outline the expected achievements of B.Tech Computer Engineering graduates within a few years post-graduation.

PEO1
Excel in technical roles, applying computer engineering knowledge to real-world challenges.
PEO2
Pursue advanced studies or leadership roles, demonstrating adaptability.
PEO3
Contribute to society through innovative, sustainable solutions.
PEO4
Uphold ethical standards and professional integrity in careers.

Program Specific Outcomes (PSO)

PSOs are tailored to the B.Tech in Computer Engineering program, focusing on specialized competencies.

PSO1
Design, develop, and test software using modern programming paradigms.
PSO2
Develop efficient computer systems with hardware-software integration.
PSO3
Apply AI, cloud computing, and cybersecurity knowledge to innovate.

Course Outcomes (CO)

COs specify learning outcomes for courses in the B.Tech Computer Engineering program.

CS101: Data Structures
  • CO1: Understand arrays, stacks, and queues.
  • CO2: Implement sorting and searching algorithms.
  • CO3: Analyze algorithm time and space complexity.
CS102: Database Management Systems
  • CO1: Design relational database schemas.
  • CO2: Write SQL queries for data manipulation.
  • CO3: Apply normalization for database optimization.
CS103: Operating Systems
  • CO1: Explain processes and threads concepts.
  • CO2: Implement scheduling algorithms.
  • CO3: Manage memory and file systems.
CS104: Computer Networks
  • CO1: Understand TCP/IP protocols.
  • CO2: Configure basic network setups.
  • CO3: Analyze network performance and security.
CS105: Software Engineering
  • CO1: Apply SDLC models.
  • CO2: Design UML diagrams for modeling.
  • CO3: Implement testing for software quality.

Admission FAQs

Find answers to common questions related to the admission process at Brahma Valley University.

Eligibility varies by course. For undergraduate programs, you typically need to have passed 12th grade with required subjects. For postgraduate programs, a relevant bachelor's degree is necessary.

You can apply online through our official admission portal. Simply fill out the application form, upload the required documents, and pay the application fee.

Some programs require entrance exams like CET, NEET, or university-conducted tests, while others may offer direct admission based on merit.

Documents typically include mark sheets, certificates, ID proof, photos, and any entrance test scorecards. Refer to our Documents Required section for the complete list.

Yes, you can apply for multiple courses. However, you need to fill out separate application forms for each course.

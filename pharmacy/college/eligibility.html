Eligibility Criteria

Discover the academic qualifications required to join Brahma Valley College of Pharmacy.

B.Pharm Admission Eligibility

Please ensure you meet the following requirements before applying for the Bachelor of Pharmacy program.

Academic Qualifications

  • Passed HSC (10+2) or equivalent examination with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology or Mathematics (PCB/PCM).
  • Minimum 45% marks in PCB/PCM for General Category candidates.
  • Minimum 40% marks in PCB/PCM for candidates from Reserved Categories (as per government norms).
  • Must appear in the applicable entrance exam (MHT-CET / NEET / equivalent), if required by the regulatory body.
Age Requirement

Candidates must be at least 17 years old on or before 31st December of the admission year.

Additional Notes

Admissions are based on merit and seat availability in accordance with DTE and PCI norms and regulations.

Required Documents

Essential documents for B.Pharmacy and D.Pharmacy admissions – Academic Year 2025–26

List of Required Documents

Sr. No Document Name
1 10th Marksheet
2 10th Passing Certificate
3 MHT-CET/NEET 2025 Non-Zero Score
4 12th Marksheet
5 12th Passing Certificate
6 Leaving Certificate
7 Domicile / Birth Certificate
8 Nationality Certificate
9 Caste Certificate
10 Caste Validity Certificate
11 Non-Creamy Layer Certificate
12 Family Income Certificate & Aadhar/PAN Card
13 Bank Passbook / Account Details
14 Ration Card

Ensure all documents are original with photocopies at the time of admission.

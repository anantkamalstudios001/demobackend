General Secretary’s Desk

Meet the visionaries guiding Brahma Valley Pharmacy toward excellence

From the General Secretary’s Desk

Mr. Gaurav Rajaram Patil

General Secretary, NGSPM’s Brahma Valley Education Campus

Dear Students,

A warm welcome to NGSPM’s Brahma Valley Institute of Pharmacy, Anjaneri, Nashik. It is a matter of great pride that since its establishment in 2023, our institute has been making steady progress in pharmaceutical education. Our dedicated faculty and vibrant student community are the heart of this institution, shaping a bright future for all.

At Brahma Valley Institute of Pharmacy (BVIOP), we are committed to delivering quality-based, value-driven education over the next four years. Our academic excellence, coupled with research, sports, student development programs, and NSS activities, ensures a holistic learning experience. Through the tireless efforts of our faculty, staff, and visionary leadership, BVIOP is emerging as a center of excellence.

I extend my best wishes to our students and staff for their future endeavors, confident that together we will achieve great heights.

Mr. Gaurav Rajaram Patil
